# Task: add a radar chart comparing suppliers to a report

`input/supplier-assessment.docx` is a short assessment of three packaging suppliers. Section 2, "Scores", says the panel's scores "are compared in the chart below", but there is no chart. The scores are in `input/supplier-scores.csv`: one row per criterion, one column per supplier, each from 1 to 5.

Insert the chart:

- A **radar (spider) chart** directly after the paragraph that announces it ("The panel scored each supplier from 1 to 5 on five criteria..."), before the paragraph that follows it.
- The criteria as the chart's categories (its spokes), in the CSV's order, and one series per supplier, named as in the CSV, with the CSV's scores exactly. The chart's own data (what Word shows under Edit Data) must agree with what it draws.
- **A legend** naming the suppliers.
- **A caption** below the chart, numbered as Word numbers figures: "Figure 1: Supplier scores by criterion, 1 to 5".
- The chart fits within the page's text width. Change nothing else.

Save the result as `work/supplier-assessment.docx`.
