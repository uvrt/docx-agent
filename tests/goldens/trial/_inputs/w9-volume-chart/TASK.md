# Task: add a chart of monthly volumes to a report

`input/operations-report.docx` is a quarterly operations report. Section 2, "Volumes", says that monthly shipment volumes "are shown in the chart below", but there is no chart. The figures are in `input/monthly-volumes.csv`: inbound and outbound volumes for January to September 2026, in thousands of units.

Insert the chart:

- A **line chart** directly after the paragraph that announces it ("Monthly shipment volumes for January to September are shown in the chart below..."), before the paragraph that follows it.
- One line per column of the CSV (Inbound, Outbound), named as in the CSV, with the months as categories in the CSV's order and the CSV's values exactly. The chart's own data (what Word shows under Edit Data) must agree with what it draws.
- **Axis titles:** "Month" on the category axis and "Thousand units" on the value axis.
- **A caption** below the chart, numbered as Word numbers figures: "Figure 1: Monthly shipment volumes, January to September 2026".
- The chart fits within the page's text width. Change nothing else.

Save the result as `work/operations-report.docx`.
